Eyes onLEADS

This app's browser can't open the portal

Google blocks sign-in inside this app, so the portal shows a blank page here. Open it in your phone's real browser.

Open in Chrome
  1. Tap ⋯ (or the share icon) at the top or bottom of this screen.
  2. Choose Open in Safari / Open in browser.

Then paste it into Safari or Chrome.

Before you enter

  1. Use your Ateneo account (@student.ateneo.edu). A personal Gmail can't apply.
  2. Signed in to more than one Google account on this browser? Google opens the portal with your first one. Use the buttons under "More than one account?" below.
  3. On a shared computer, use a private / incognito window.
Enter the portal

"Sorry, unable to open the file" or "You need access"? That's Google mixing up your accounts, not you. Try the buttons below.

More than one account?

Google numbers the accounts on this browser in the order you signed in (the list you see when you tap your profile picture in Gmail). Tap the number of your Ateneo account:

Not sure which number? Try them in order. Surest fix: a private / incognito window, signed in to your Ateneo account only.
