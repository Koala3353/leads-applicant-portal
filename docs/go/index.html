This app's browser can't open the portal

Google blocks sign-in inside this app, so the portal shows a blank page here. Open it in your phone's real browser.

Open in Chrome
  1. Tap ⋯ (or the share icon) at the top or bottom of this screen.
  2. Choose Open in Safari / Open in browser.

Then paste it into Safari or Chrome.

Before you enter

  1. Use your Ateneo account (@student.ateneo.edu). A personal Gmail can't apply.
  2. Signed in to more than one Google account on this browser? Google opens the portal with your first one. See "More than one account?" below.
  3. On a shared computer, use a private / incognito window.
Enter the portal

"Sorry, unable to open the file" or "You need access"? That's Google mixing up your accounts, not you. See below.

More than one account?

Google opens the portal with the first account signed in on this browser, so a second Ateneo or personal account can get "Sorry, unable to open the file". Any one of these fixes it:

  1. Private window: open a private / incognito window, sign in with your Ateneo account only, and paste this link there.
  2. Another browser: use a browser where only your Ateneo account is signed in (e.g. Chrome if Safari has your other accounts).
  3. Sign out of the others on this browser, then tap Enter the portal again.
